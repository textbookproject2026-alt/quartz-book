# Example concept

A concept page: one idea, explained on its own, in a few sentences. Chapters
reach these pages through wikilinks such as `[[Example concept]]`, and the
reading site shows them on hover.

The filename is the link target, so it is prose — capitals and spaces are
correct here, and a rename breaks every chapter that links to it.

Delete this file once you have concept pages of your own. If you have none,
delete the `Definitions` folder and the `concepts` collection in
`templates/admin/config.yml` with it.
